Course Schedule

Weekly schedule

Please note: this schedule is meant to provide an outline of the semester and is tentative. The pace of the course may deviate from the exact schedule outlined below. Any changes will be announced in class and posted here.

Friday September 18, 2026 Exam 1

WEEK 7
Monday October 5, 2026 Probability continued: independence, random variables and discrete probability distributions
Wednesday October 7, 2026 Discrete distributions continued: expected value, Bernoulli and binomial distributions

Monday October 12, 2026 Sampling distributions and the standard error
Wednesday October 14, 2026 Exam 2 Review
Friday October 16, 2026 Exam 2 (take-home, open-note, in Canvas) – due Sunday, October 18 at 11:59PM

Monday October 19, 2026 Finding normal probabilities; the central limit theorem; confidence intervals for a population mean and a population proportion
Wednesday October 21, 2026 Confidence intervals continued: confidence levels other than 95%
Friday October 23, 2026 Intervals for a mean when σ is unknown (the t distribution) – Course project check due Sunday, October 25 at 11:59PM

Monday October 26, 2026 Intervals for a mean when σ is unknown, concluded; choosing a minimum sample size; introduction to significance tests
Wednesday October 28, 2026 Significance tests: hypotheses, test statistics and P-values; tests for a population proportion
Friday October 30, 2026 Tests for a population mean – October 31 is the last day to withdraw (W) or withdraw to audit (WA)
WEEK 11
Monday November 2, 2026 Composite hypotheses, relationship between confidence intervals and hypothesis tests, the sign test
Wednesday November 4, 2026 Making decisions, types of errors in hypothesis tests, statistical power
Friday November 6, 2026 Introduction to two-sample tests, independent vs dependent samples – Homework 3 due Sunday, November 8 at 11:59PM

Monday November 9, 2026 Tests for comparing two independent proportions or two independent means
Wednesday November 11, 2026 Tests for dependent samples, causal inference
